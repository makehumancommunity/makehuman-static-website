The MakeHuman community is centered around the MakeHuman core assets (such as the Basemesh), the tools that operates on these (such as
MPFB and MakeHuman) and the community assets which extend and enhance them.

## MakeHuman 2

[MakeHuman 2](https://makehuman-2.github.io) is the spiritual successor of the original MakeHuman GUI application.
It is run as a standalone desktop application, and is intependent of Blender.

## MPFB

[MPFB]({{% relref "../MPFB" %}}) is a reimplementation of MakeHuman as a Blender extension. It provides a full character creation
suite inside Blender.

## MakeHuman 1.x

[MakeHuman 1.x]({{% relref "../MakeHuman" %}}) is the original GUI applications. While it is still available, it is generally considered
deprecated in favor of either MakeHuman 2 or MPFB.
