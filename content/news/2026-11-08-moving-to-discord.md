---
title: "Community is moving to discord"
date: 2026-11-08
---

The forums have been shut down permanently, as there isn't any hope left that they will come back online. 

Instead, we're moving on to discord, re-using the [MakeHuman2 discord instance](https://discord.gg/tnhDFdVFyC).

We're working on a replacement for the asset repository, but there's no immediate solution available.

Note that the asset files as such are still online, they're just not browseable. The master metadata file
is in [a json file](https://files.makehumancommunity.org/sites/default/files/assets.json). By reading this
you can find URLs to all existing assets. With some scripting or by manually copy/pasting addresses, you can 
download any asset which was previously available via the asset repository.

Or you can go for the [Asset Packs]({{% relref "../Assets/AssetPacks" %}}) which were unaffected by all this.
